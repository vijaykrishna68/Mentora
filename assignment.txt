The brief · from Disha :
We are looking for curious minds who enjoy building things and thinking beyond the obvious. Your goal is to deliver a production ready app.

Build a small Appointment Booking application where a user can:

- View available appointment slots
- Select and book a slot
- View their upcoming/past appointments
- Cancel an appointment

The application should include a polished frontend (1-2 screens are sufficient), backend APIs and a database. You are free to choose the technology stack, architecture and approach. You may use AI tools during development.



we are evaluating much more than whether the application works.

1. Product & UX
- Intuitive and frictionless user experience
- Thoughtful information hierarchy
- Excellent UI/UX and visual polish
- Responsive design
- Loading, empty, error and success states
- Clear feedback for user actions
- Attention to small details and overall product quality

The final experience should feel like something you would be comfortable shipping to real users.

2. Engineering
- Clean, maintainable and well-structured code
- Good API and data-model design
- Proper validation and error handling
- Reliability and data consistency
- Handling of concurrent booking attempts
- Appropriate authentication/authorization
- Meaningful automated tests

3. Product thinking
The requirements are intentionally kept simple and do not specify every scenario. You do not need to build a large application. Keep the scope small, but make the quality high. We are particularly interested in seeing whether you can take a simple requirement and turn it into something that feels thought-through, polished and production-ready.
