Design Language v1

Fluid, restrained neo‑brutalism.

A warm paper canvas, near‑black type, and one disciplined accent. The brutalist character comes from borders, contrast and one physical shadow gesture, reserved for the moments that matter: choosing a slot, confirming a booking.

North star

Bold enough to be memorable. Calm enough to trust. Fluid enough to feel alive. Simple enough to book without thinking.

Color

A three-part foundation — warm paper, near-black ink, one signature accent — plus a semantic set kept strictly separate from the brand. Signal Blue was chosen over a softer indigo: it reads more electric against warm paper without tipping into a generic SaaS blue.
